QUICK GUIDE

What makes an AVL tree special?

An AVL tree is a Binary Search Tree that automatically keeps its height small after insertions and deletions.

01

Why balance matters

A normal BST can become a chain when values arrive in sorted order. Search may then take O(n) time. AVL balancing keeps operations at O(log n).

02

Balance factor

BF = height(left) − height(right). A node is balanced when its factor is −1, 0, or +1.

03

Four rotations

LL: right rotation. RR: left rotation. LR: left rotation on the child, then right rotation. RL: right rotation on the child, then left rotation.

04

AVL vs Red-Black

AVL trees are more strictly balanced and can suit lookup-heavy workloads. Red-Black trees allow looser balance and can perform well when updates are frequent.
